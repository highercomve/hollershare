MAKE THE CONNECTION

A file’s next stop.
In three small steps.

  1. 01

    Get a little closer.

    Keep both devices on the same Wi-Fi network. Open HollerShare, or enable receiving in Android Quick Share.

  2. 02

    Pick something good.

    Choose files, or paste and review text. Select the nearby device you want to send to.

  3. 03

    Let it travel.

    Accept the request on the receiving device. Files land in its received-files folder; accepted text is copied to its clipboard.
