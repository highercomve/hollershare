## Short description

Share files and text directly with nearby computers and Android devices.

## Full description

From here to over there.

HollerShare makes it easy to share files and text with nearby computers and Android devices using Quick Share. Keep your devices on the same local network, choose something to send, and accept it on the other device.

SHARE THE GOOD STUFF
• Send files to nearby devices.
• Paste or type text, review it, and send it directly.
• Receive files into the folder you choose.
• Accepted incoming text is copied to your clipboard automatically.

MAKE THE CONNECTION
• Discover nearby devices over the local network, with Bluetooth discovery support.
• Share with HollerShare on Linux, Windows and macOS, or compatible Android Quick Share devices.
• Choose how your device appears to others with a custom device name.

A CLEAR DEAL
• No signup or cloud account required.
• No advertising or analytics.
• Encrypted Quick Share transfers between devices.
• Free and open source.

Both devices need a reachable local-network connection. Nearby-device permissions help discovery. Transfers require acceptance on the receiving device. Bluetooth helps devices find each other; transfer contents travel over the network.

HollerShare is an independent open-source application. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or Samsung.
